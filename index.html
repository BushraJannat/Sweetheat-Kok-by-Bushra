WELCOME TO MY KITCHEN

Where Asian Flavours Meet European Inspiration

Welcome to Sweetheat Kök by Bushra, a personal cooking portfolio inspired by the flavours of Asia and the creativity of European cuisine.

My cooking is a journey between different food cultures. I love exploring traditional Bangladeshi and Asian dishes while also learning from European food, ingredients and presentation.

Sometimes I follow traditional recipes, and sometimes I create my own ideas by bringing different flavours together. From comforting Bangladeshi dishes and spicy Asian food to colourful desserts and European-inspired creations, Sweetheat Kök is a place where familiar flavours meet new ideas.

A little taste of Asia, a touch of Europe.
