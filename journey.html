MY STORY

My Cooking Journey

My love for cooking started when I was young. What began as a small hobby slowly became a passion, a creative space and a way to connect with my roots.

Childhood — The Beginning

My love for cooking started when I was young. I grew up watching my mother cook, and sometimes I would try to help her or make small dishes myself. At that time, cooking was simply something I enjoyed doing. I never imagined that one day it would become such an important part of my life.

2013 — A More Serious Beginning

In 2013, after finishing college, my interest in cooking started to grow. I bought a popular Bangladeshi recipe book from a book fair, and that book became an important part of my early cooking journey.

I started trying different recipes at home by following the instructions in the book. At first, I cooked simply because I enjoyed it. Every new recipe was a small experiment, and every successful dish made me excited to try something new.

After 2013 — From a Hobby to a Family Favourite

As I continued cooking, my family began to notice that I was becoming quite good at it. Whenever we had guests at home, my mother would often prepare and organise the ingredients for me, while I would take care of the cooking.

I was never particularly good at all the cutting and preparation — I definitely needed some help there! But once I picked up the cooking spoon, I knew what I wanted to do.

My family started enjoying my food so much that whenever guests were coming, they would often tell me, “You cook it — you make it really delicious!” That encouragement gave me confidence and made me want to learn even more.

Sweden — Cooking With Love

Later, when I moved to Sweden and started living together with my husband, cooking became even more special to me.

I often cooked for him simply because I enjoyed it. Sometimes I prepared his favourite dishes, and sometimes I tried something new to surprise him.

My husband has also helped me a lot in the kitchen, especially with cutting and preparing ingredients. In a funny way, we became a little cooking team — he helped with the preparation, while I focused on bringing the flavours together.

Flavour in Every Bite

As I cooked more and more, I realised what I love most about cooking.

Whenever I pick up my cooking spoon, my goal is to bring flavour into every single bite.

For me, cooking is not just about following a recipe. I love tasting the food while I cook, adjusting the spices, balancing different flavours and thinking about how each ingredient can make the dish better.

I want every bite to have something special.

“You Have Magic in Your Hands!”

Today, I often receive a funny but very heartwarming compliment from my family and friends: “You have magic in your hands!”

They jokingly say that whatever I cook somehow turns out delicious and full of flavour. It always makes me smile.

I know they are giving me a compliment, but honestly, those words really inspire me. Every time someone enjoys my food or asks for another serving, it motivates me to keep learning, experimenting and becoming better.

From Hobby to Passion

What started as a small hobby slowly became something much bigger. Cooking became my creative space, a way to connect with my roots, express myself and show love to the people around me.

Today, I enjoy exploring the connection between Asian heritage and European inspiration. I love traditional Bangladeshi and Asian flavours, but I also enjoy discovering European dishes, ingredients and presentation.

From watching my mother cook as a child, to buying my first recipe book in 2013, cooking for my family, creating surprises for my husband, and now exploring Euro-Asian fusion — my cooking journey has grown and changed with me.
